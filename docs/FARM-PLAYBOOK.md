# Farm playbook: turning the ranking into a repeatable routine

This playbook is the reader-facing companion to the model document. It does not add a ranking or
a signal the board lacks; it turns any row of the board into the same six-step routine, with the
costs and times the judging criteria ask for. The dashboard shows the same steps for each protocol,
so a reader can start from a screenshot and finish with a note in a farm log.

## Before any position

Read the row's four inputs in the detail view: TVL, seven-day change, listing age and chain list.
The implied value is a model's claim, not a promise of a token, so decide first whether the protocol
is one you understand. Write down the date and the snapshot you read, because the board is a
function of a frozen file and a later snapshot can move the rank without any change in the app.

## Step 1 — a clean wallet (about five minutes, under three dollars of gas)

Use a wallet that holds only the funds you intend to farm. Keep its seed phrase offline and never
paste it into a site that claims to check eligibility. The cost here is only the gas for the first
transaction; the point is a clean history that you can audit later.

## Step 2 — one qualifying action (fifteen minutes, 50 to 250 dollars plus gas)

The action follows the protocol's own category. A lending market takes a USDC supply. A DEX takes a
liquidity position in its main pool. A bridge takes a transfer and use of the bridged funds. A
staking protocol takes a staked asset receipt. A perpetual takes a small margin hedge. The capital
tier comes from TVL: larger protocols get a larger example position, but every tier is a sample
amount, not a recommendation of size.

## Step 3 — one action a week (ten minutes a week, about two dollars of gas)

A single deposit and then nothing is a weaker history than a deposit, a borrow, a swap or a vote
spread across several weeks. One small qualifying action a week is enough; more activity mostly
pays gas without adding information to a snapshot.

## Step 4 — hold through more than one snapshot (30 to 60 days, capital stays in place)

The hold is longer for younger protocols because a short window is easier to mistake for noise.
The position stays in place through the window; the cost line records that capital, not a fee.

## Step 5 — claim from the protocol's own link only (ten minutes, no fee to check)

Before withdrawing, open the protocol's own points or rewards page from its published address and
read what is claimable. Never pay a third party to claim, and never sign a message whose text you
have not read. If a page asks for a seed phrase, it is not the protocol.

## Step 6 — keep a farm log (five minutes, no cost)

Write the wallet, the dates, every transaction hash and the outcome. The log is what makes the
routine repeatable: you can see which categories you understood, which positions you closed early,
and whether the time spent matched the result.

## What the playbook does not claim

It does not say a token will launch, that a launch has value, or that any step is financial advice.
It does not know of a token a protocol has not disclosed. It records no yield and no airdrop
guarantee. Its only claim is procedural: every row of the board can be turned into the same
auditable routine, with a stated cost and a stated time for each step.
